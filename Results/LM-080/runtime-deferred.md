LM-080's local diagnostics implementation is code-complete. Safe evidence proves the typed
content-free record schema, fixed log bounds, owner-only permissions, symlink rejection,
revalidated export, health projection, explicit/nonpolling refresh model, signpost wiring,
and application compile closure. It does not prove live overhead, rendered dashboards, or
installed-product behavior.

H9 must run the exact signed revision on the physically distinct isolated validation Mac:

1. Exercise capture admission, enrichment, search, and diagnostic export while recording
   signpost duration/overlap and compare CPU, energy, resident memory, storage growth, and
   index backlog with diagnostics enabled and disabled. Preserve the original release
   budgets and prove diagnostics add no material regression.
2. Fill and rotate all three log files during normal and failure paths. Confirm each file
   remains at most 1 MiB, every record at most 8 KiB, permissions remain `0700`/`0600`, and
   no pixels, text, titles, URLs, queries, agent results, audio, or transcripts appear.
3. Open the installed Diagnostics pane and verify capture status, energy, resident memory,
   storage, monthly-growth baseline, index backlog, error state, refresh, and export in
   nominal, constrained, empty, loading, and failure cases.
4. Use keyboard and VoiceOver at default/minimum window sizes and light, dark, and Increased
   Contrast appearances. Confirm labels and health state do not rely on color alone.
5. Create a diagnostic bundle, inspect its preview before reveal, independently validate
   every JSON/JSONL record, and prove the bundle stays local and content-free under deny-all
   outbound instrumentation.

No application, ScreenCaptureKit, Apple ImageIO, VideoToolbox, software HEIC helper, or
hardware-media runtime was executed on this laptop. A passing H9-020 ledger item is required
before LM-080 can be promoted to passed.
